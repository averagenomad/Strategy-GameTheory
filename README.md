## **Overview**  
This repository contains the materials from a group project exploring the Venezuelan crisis using **game theory, decision theory, spatial bargaining, scenario and stakeholder analyses**.  

The project was designed as an **academic exercise** and uses game-theoretic approaches to evaluate strategic courses of action (COAs) that could be taken by the United States and its allies against Nicolás Maduro's regime.  

### **Key Components of the Analysis**  
- **SWOT analysis** of the Venezuelan crisis.  
- **Stakeholder identification and prioritization** based on influence and importance.  
- Development of **decision trees**, **spatial bargaining models**, and **game-theoretic payoffs**.  
- Comparative evaluation of potential strategies:
  - Do nothing  
  - Diplomacy  
  - Sanctions  
  - Instigate coup  
  - Military invasion  

> ⚠️ **Note:** This was a **school group project** and does not represent formal policy recommendations or a published paper.

## **Highlights**  
- Applied **spatial bargaining** to identify potential winsets between stakeholders.  
- Used **hedging and minimax strategies** to evaluate the least-risk COA across scenarios.  
- Considered two primary scenarios:
  1. **High COVID virulence** (pandemic uncertainty)  
  2. **Oil price recovery** (economic strengthening of the regime)
